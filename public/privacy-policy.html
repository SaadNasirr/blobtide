Blobtide Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 September 2026

Blobtide (“the game”, “we”) is a one-thumb crowd runner published as a mobile and web game. This policy explains what data is processed and why.

Data we process

  • Local save data: coins, unlocked skins, current level, and an ads-removed flag. Stored on device (or browser localStorage on web).
  • Advertising identifiers: if you play a store build with ads enabled, Google AdMob (and optional mediation) may collect the advertising ID, IP address, device type, and coarse location to serve and measure ads. On iOS we request App Tracking Transparency before tracking.
  • Purchases: Google Play Billing / Apple In-App Purchase process payments. We receive a purchase token/SKU, not your full card number.
  • Crash and analytics (optional): Firebase Crashlytics / Unity Analytics / Google Analytics may collect crash stacks, country, OS version, and event names such as level_start, level_win, rewarded_continue. We do not ask for your name or email in v1.
  • Consent: in the EEA/UK we use Google UMP to collect consent for personalized ads where required.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information. We do not design the game for children under 13 (not COPPA-directed). Do not create an account; there is no user profile server in v1.

Retention

Local saves remain until you uninstall the app or clear site data. Ad and analytics partners retain data under their own policies.

Partners

  • Google AdMob / Google Play / Google Analytics
  • Apple App Store / App Store Server
  • Unity Technologies (if you play a Unity build)

Your choices

  • Buy Remove Ads to stop third-party ads in the game client.
  • iOS: deny tracking in the ATT prompt.
  • Android: reset or opt out of ads in device settings.
  • EEA: manage consent via the in-game privacy message.
